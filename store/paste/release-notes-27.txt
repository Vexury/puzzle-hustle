<en-US>
Hustle: an endless mode across all ten puzzles.

- Hustle: one puzzle after another, harder as you climb. Badges, flairs and your level in groups
- Hints: one free hint a day, then coins or a short video
- Every level ships ready-made, hard Zip opens instantly. 30 levels per pack, renumbered
- Tracks: a train runs the solved line. You draw the last track yourself again
- New Profile: Social, Stats and settings on their own pages
- Swipe to switch tabs
</en-US>
