Good news: eight new puzzles are waiting for you, and there will be eight more tomorrow.

Puzzle Hustle is for the kind of person who reads "one crown per row, column and region, and they may never touch" and immediately wants to have a go. Nonograms, Sudoku, Killer Sudoku, Crowns, Stars, Mosaic, Zip and Shapes, all in one app, all refreshed every single day.

And every single one plays fair. Each puzzle goes through a solver before it ever reaches you, so there is exactly one answer and a clean line of reasoning that gets you there. No guessing. No filling in a corner, crossing your fingers, and finding out ten minutes later that it was wrong all along. When you are stuck, the next step really is sitting there waiting to be spotted. That little click in your head when you find it is the whole reason we built this.

YOUR DAILY EIGHT
One puzzle of every type, grown from today's date, which means your Tuesday puzzles are everyone else's Tuesday puzzles. Solve three and your streak rolls on. There is a chunkier Weekly and a properly big Monthly for the days you want to settle in with a coffee.

WHAT YOU GET TO SOLVE
- Shapes: slide the pieces around until the lit pattern matches the target
- Nonogram: paint the hidden picture from the numbers, black and white or in colour
- Mosaic: every number tells you how many cells around it are filled
- Crowns: one crown per row, column and region, and they hate being neighbours
- Stars: same deal, now with two stars per line
- Sudoku: you know this one
- Killer Sudoku: Sudoku, plus cages that have to add up
- Zip: draw a single path through every cell, reaching the numbers in order

PLAY AS MUCH AS YOU LIKE
Twenty levels for every type and difficulty, fifty for Shapes, lined up from gentle warm-up to genuinely genius. Run out? Endless random puzzles are waiting in four difficulties.

Pencil marks for the Sudoku crowd. Undo for the bold. A hint for when you have earned it. Your streak, your progress and that half-finished board you abandoned on the bus are all still there when you come back. Light and dark theme, whichever suits the hour.

Everything is generated right on your phone, so trains, planes and basements are all fine.

No account, no sign-up, no "create a profile to continue". Open it and go solve something.
