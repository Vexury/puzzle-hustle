• Cats: one cat per row, column and colour, and they refuse to be neighbours
• Hearts: same idea, now with two hearts per line
• Zip: draw a single path through every cell, reaching the numbers in order
• Shapes: slide the pieces until the lit pattern matches the target
• Tracks: lay one track from A to B, the numbers count the rails in each line
• Slabs: place every slab so each coloured region follows its rule
