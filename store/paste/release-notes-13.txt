New buttons, Redo, and a phone that answers your taps.

- Haptic feedback, switchable in Profile
- Icon buttons under the board, Redo next to Undo
- Nonogram and Mosaic mark clues red that can no longer fit
- Mosaic numbers grey out once enough cells are filled
- Started puzzles say Continue, levels jump to your next one
- Solved dailies show your finished board
- Times from before signing in reach the leaderboard
- The puzzle holds still while a hint video loads
