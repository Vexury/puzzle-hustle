<en-US>
A new Social page and fewer slow puzzles in Hustle.

- Social: swipe through the puzzles, group actions sit in the group menu
- Standings: your name first, up to three badges and your flair below it
- Hustle: Sudoku comes every 25th stage, Sumdoku every 50th, as boss levels
- Hints in Cats and Hearts always place a symbol
- Hearts Medium: new levels without giveaway patterns; unfinished Hearts puzzles start over
- Reset sits next to Hint
- After a solve, your rank opens the standings
</en-US>
